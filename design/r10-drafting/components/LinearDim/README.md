# LinearDim

A linear dimension: extension lines with a small gap from the object, a dimension line with closed filled arrowheads, and the measurement in Simplex above it, all in ACI 2 by default.

Provide the two points (`x1 y1 x2 y2`), `direction` ("horizontal" or "vertical"), `offset` (px from the object; negative is above) and `text` (defaults to the length in units of `unit` px, four decimals).

- Four decimal places, no unit suffix — the era's default dimension style.
